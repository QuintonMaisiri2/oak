Suggestion: Meet other partners. 
Orderly speed networking format. This involves brief, timed conversations where participants quickly exchange key information about themselves or their organizations before moving on to the next person. It keeps interactions dynamic, efficient and encourages meeting many people in a short time.

Morning plenary — keynote / thematic framing

Suggestion: Beyond funding, building partnerships 

Panel discussion: vibrant exchange of 3 people 
It encourages multiple perspectives and real-world examples.
Sparks dynamic conversation and audience engagement.
Sets an inspiring, practical tone for the day's discussions.

10:15 – 12:00 
Breakout sessions (parallel tracks, by theme or portfolio area)
5 themed tracks running concurrently, each carrying equal weight so no single topic dominates
Each room facilitated by a chosen presenter leading the discussion
Participants choose their track in advance via the registration form, based on relevance to their work 
Each track closes with 2–3 key takeaways captured for the closing reflections

Suggested tracks: 
Innovative Financing Models for Sustainable Development: Exploring alternatives like social impact bonds, blended finance, and crowdfunding.
Building Stronger Multi-Sector Partnerships: Strategies for collaboration between governments, NGOs, the private sector, and communities
Leveraging Technology to Enhance Partnership Outcomes:
Using digital tools and data for transparency, monitoring, and scaling impact.
Addressing Policy and Regulatory Challenges: Navigating local policies affecting funding flows and partnerships.
Capacity Building for Local Organizations: Empowering grassroots groups to sustain partnerships beyond funding cycles.
Measuring Impact Beyond Financial Metrics: Developing effective ways to assess the social and community impact of partnerships.
Sustainability and Environmental Considerations in Partnerships: Integrating climate resilience and sustainability into partnership models.
Innovating Community Engagement and Co-Creation: Engaging communities as active partners in designing and implementing projects


12:00 – 13:00 
Partner showcase—brief lightning updates from selected partners
Feature 5 partners from across the network's key focus areas (Thriving Families & Communities, Skills & Entrepreneurship, Special Needs, and Organizational Strengthening) to showcase the diversity, impact, and innovation of the 100+ partner network. 
Proposed format: short video features per partner, showing their work in their own words.
Selection criteria (for discussion/decision):
Impact: Partners with strong, measurable outcomes or standout success stories
Diversity: Mix of sectors, geographies, and organization types (NGO, private sector, grassroots)
Innovation: Partners using creative or new approaches to their work
Relevance: Alignment with the conference theme or current strategic priorities
Collaboration: Active engagement in knowledge-sharing across the network
